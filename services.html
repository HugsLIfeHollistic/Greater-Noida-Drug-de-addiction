Our services

Tailored, evidence-based programmes for lasting recovery from addiction and mental health challenges.

Family counselling

Addiction never happens in isolation — it reshapes the whole family. Our family counselling programme is built to repair relationships, rebuild trust, and turn loved ones into active partners in recovery.

Sessions are led by experienced family therapists who understand the cultural and social context Indian families are working within.

1

Initial family assessment

Understanding family dynamics, codependency patterns and trauma history.

2

Joint therapy sessions

Structured sessions with the patient and family for open, guided communication.

3

Boundaries & communication training

Practical tools for healthy boundaries and relapse prevention at home.

4

Ongoing family support groups

Regular group meetings for families navigating the recovery journey together.

What you can expect

  • ✓Family therapists with addiction specialisation
  • ✓Sessions available in Hindi and English
  • ✓Flexible scheduling for working families
  • ✓Complete confidentiality and privacy
  • ✓Monthly family education workshops
  • ✓Post-discharge follow-up for one year

Rehabilitation services

Our residential programme gives people a structured, nurturing environment to heal in — away from triggers, with clinical professionals, counsellors and peers close by every day.

🛏️
Residential stay

Clean, comfortable rooms with 24/7 care and security.

🧘
Holistic therapies

Daily yoga, meditation and breathing practice.

📚
Life skills training

Vocational guidance and social skills rebuilding.

🤝
Peer support

Group therapy and mentorship from alumni.

🍱
Nutritional care

Dietician-planned, freshly cooked meals.

📋
Aftercare planning

A personalised post-discharge and follow-up plan.

Programme structure

1

Detoxification (1–2 weeks)

Medically supervised withdrawal management in a clinical setting.

2

Intensive therapy (4–8 weeks)

Daily individual and group therapy, CBT and motivational interviewing.

3

Reintegration (2–4 weeks)

A gradual return to daily responsibilities with continued support.

4

Aftercare (12 months)

Outpatient follow-ups, alumni groups and family sessions.

Drug addiction programme

Our flagship de-addiction programme is built on the ASAM framework, combining medical management, evidence-based psychotherapy, holistic wellness and long-term aftercare, all under one roof.

🏥
Medical detoxification

24/7 medically supervised detox with support to manage withdrawal safely.

🧠
Dual diagnosis treatment

Treating addiction and co-occurring conditions like depression or anxiety together.

💬
Cognitive behavioural therapy

Identifying and changing the thought patterns that fuel substance use.

🔄
Relapse prevention

SMART Recovery and 12-step work, adapted for the Indian context.

🌿
Holistic healing

Yoga, art and music therapy, naturopathy and spiritual counselling.

📱
Aftercare & alumni network

Monthly follow-up calls and a dedicated helpline after discharge.

Substances we treat

Alcohol Heroin & opioids Cannabis Cocaine Methamphetamine Prescription drug abuse Tobacco & nicotine Behavioural addictions
