How to reach us

Greater Noida is well-connected by road and metro from Delhi, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad.

  • 🚇Metro: Aqua Line, nearest station by auto-rickshaw
  • 🚗By road: Via Yamuna Expressway and NH-09
  • ✈️From Delhi airport: ~45–60 min via Yamuna Expressway
  • 🚂From railway stations: Hazrat Nizamuddin / New Delhi, 45 min by road

About the Dehradun branch

Our Dehradun centre serves the Uttarakhand region with the same standard of holistic care as our flagship Greater Noida centre, in a quieter Himalayan-foothill setting.

🚗
How to reach Dehradun
  • ✈️By air: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (30 min drive)
  • 🚂By train: Dehradun Railway Station, well-connected from Delhi
  • 🚌By road: NH 7 from Delhi (~6 hours); buses and taxis available
