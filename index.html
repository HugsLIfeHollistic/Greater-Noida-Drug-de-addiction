Who we are

A sanctuary of hope and healing

Hugs Life Holistic is a registered mental health and drug de-addiction rehabilitation establishment, operating with clinical expertise and genuine care. Our flagship centre is in Greater Noida, with a branch centre in Dehradun.

We treat addiction the way it actually behaves — as something physical, psychological, social and spiritual all at once — and build a recovery plan that addresses all four.
