Our difference

Why Hugs Life Holistic?

We're not just a rehabilitation centre — here's what that means in practice.

🌿

A truly holistic approach

We treat the whole person — body, mind and spirit — integrating modern medicine with yoga, Ayurveda, meditation and art therapy.

👨‍⚕️

An expert, multidisciplinary team

Psychiatrists, addiction physicians, psychologists, counsellors, yoga instructors and nutritionists, working as one team.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family-centred care

Families are involved at every stage, so home becomes a source of support rather than a trigger.

🔒

Complete confidentiality

Every admission is handled with discretion. No information is shared without consent.

🏡

A homely, safe environment

Our centre is designed to feel like a home, not a clinical institution — residents are treated with respect and dignity.

📊

Evidence-based programmes

Our protocols are grounded in validated methods including CBT, motivational interviewing and 12-step facilitation.

🇮🇳

A government-aligned initiative

As partners of the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan, we work to national standards for de-addiction care.

📅

Lifelong aftercare support

Our 12-month aftercare programme, alumni network and helpline mean recovery doesn't end at discharge.

Values that guide us

💕
Compassion first

Every resident is received with kindness and without judgement. Addiction is a disease, not a moral failing.

🎯
Individualised treatment

Every resident gets a plan built around their substance use, mental health and life goals.

🌱
Long-term recovery

We build for a sustainable life beyond addiction, not just short-term sobriety.

🤝
Transparency & trust

Open, honest communication with families throughout the treatment journey.
